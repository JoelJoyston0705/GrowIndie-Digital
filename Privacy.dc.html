2. What we collect

Only what you give us, plus a small amount of technical data our host records automatically.

Audit request form

Your name, work email, company name, website, monthly marketing budget band, and what you want to grow. Name and email are required; the rest is optional.

Newsletter signup

Your email address, nothing else.

Direct contact

If you WhatsApp, call, email, or DM us, we keep that conversation and any details you share in it.

Technical data

Our web host records standard server logs — IP address, browser, pages requested, timestamps — for security and traffic counts. Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site. This site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing on your device. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

3. Why we use it

To prepare and send the free marketing audit you asked for.

To reply to you and discuss a possible engagement.

To deliver and invoice services if you become a client.

To send occasional updates, if you signed up for them.

To keep the site secure and meet our tax and legal obligations.

We rely on your consent (given when you submit a form) and, once you are a client, on the necessity of performing our contract with you. We never use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to: tell you what we hold about you; correct or complete it; delete it; withdraw a consent you gave; and nominate someone to exercise these rights if you cannot. You can also raise a grievance with us and, if we don't resolve it, with the Data Protection Board of India.

How to exercise them

Email growindiedigital@gmail.com with the subject line "Data request". We reply within 7 working days and complete the request within 30 days. There is no charge.

7. Security

This site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. Access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it to answer you, on accounts protected by two-factor authentication. No system is perfect; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes affecting how we use data you already gave us will be emailed to you where we have your address.
